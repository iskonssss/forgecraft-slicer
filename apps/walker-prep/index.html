    In Bambu Studio

    1. Open the downloaded file (unzip it first, then open the .3mf inside). Choose your printer if it asks.
    2. Under the walker you should see the body, centre puck and leg slot support blocker. Solid bodies also get a leg block and a leg slot cut (a negative part that carves the slots and leaves the pin).
    3. Slice with the standard 0.20 mm profile. Check the preview: no support inside the two leg slots.
    4. Print the two legs at 100% infill as usual.

    Balance numbers are estimates from a 0.5 mm voxel model with 2 walls and PLA at 1.24 g/cm³. Ranges in grey come from the 11 lineup walkers. They are a guide from that set, not tested limits.
